Clearo

Snap an invoice, get a clean accounting record - capture, a review workflow, dashboards, and accountant-ready exports.

Open live site Open the demo

What I built & why

Clearo takes a photo or PDF of an invoice and turns it into a structured accounting record. It pulls out the line items, you review and correct them, assign categories, and approve. The same flow handles both purchase and sales invoices, so it covers money going out and coming in.

On top of that sits a dashboard - sales, purchases, net, and a pending-review count, with monthly and by-category charts - plus PDF and Excel export for handing off to an accountant, and a full English / Spanish interface. I built it to see how close a small, focused project could get to a genuinely useful accounting tool.

Why this stack

Clearo is a demo product, so each choice favored shipping a complete, believable app quickly over infrastructure I'd only need at scale.

  • Next.js 16 & React 19Server components render the marketing pages and the dashboard from one codebase, and the App Router keeps public and auth-gated routes cleanly separated.
  • SQLite + Drizzle ORMA single-file database is plenty for a demo, and Drizzle's typed schema keeps the queries and the TypeScript types from drifting apart.
  • better-authSession and account handling out of the box, without standing up a third-party auth service for what is still a demo.
  • Tailwind CSS v4The config-less setup got the UI moving fast, and utility classes kept the dashboard's dense layout manageable.
  • RechartsComposable React chart components covered the monthly and by-category breakdowns without a custom charting layer.
