Pooch

A MERN app that matches dog owners and helps them set up playdates.

MongoDB React Node.js
View repo

What I built & why

Pooch pairs up dog owners nearby and helps them arrange meetups for their dogs. Profiles cover the dog as much as the person - size, energy, temperament - so matches actually make sense.

Built as a MERN app with a small team: React on the front end, Express and MongoDB behind it. My focus was the matching flow and the profile data model.

Why this stack
