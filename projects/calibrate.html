Calibrate Tool

Calibrate your resume to the role - measure the fit, tune it side-by-side with AI, then export to DOCX or PDF.

React 19 Next.js 16 TypeScript Python FastAPI PyMuPDF Ollama
Open live site

What I built & why

Tailoring a resume for every application is tedious, and most "AI resume" tools rewrite you into someone you are not. Calibrate keeps your voice and your actual experience while lining the resume up with what the role asks for.

You upload your resume and the job description. It measures the fit with a match report, then opens an inline-editable side-by-side view where the AI tunes wording to your tone, keeps your formatting, and stays inside your real experience. When it looks right, you export to DOCX or PDF.

Why this stack

  • React 19 + Next.js 16The inline-editable side-by-side editor needs fine-grained, responsive state across two synced documents.
  • TypeScriptThe match report, edit model, and export payload all share one strongly-typed document schema.
  • Python FastAPIHandles parsing, scoring, and the LLM calls behind clean, documented endpoints.
  • PyMuPDFExtracts text and layout from uploaded resume PDFs precisely enough to preserve formatting on export.
  • OllamaRuns the tuning model locally, so resumes and job descriptions never leave the machine.
