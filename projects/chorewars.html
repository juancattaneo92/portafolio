ChoreWars

A touchscreen kiosk that tracks household chore streaks and resets itself every night.

Python Flask SQLite Raspberry Pi
Open live site Open the demo

What I built & why

A small kiosk mode app, always-on touchscreen screen by the door that tracks who did which chores, shows completion streaks and monthly counts, and keeps a running history for two pSeople. It auto-resets the daily list at midnight so nobody has to.

It runs on a Raspberry Pi, Flask serving a full-screen page, SQLite holding the history. The whole thing is deliberately small: one device, one file, no cloud. Your data safe.

Why this stack

  • PythonFast to prototype and the richest ecosystem for OCR, ML, and glue code.
  • FlaskMinimal footprint for a single-purpose kiosk app, just routing and templates, nothing else to manage.
  • SQLiteA single file database is all a one-device kiosk needs, and it survives power cycles cleanly.
  • Raspberry PiFun, inexpensive, GPIO-friendly hardware that is a realistic target for a small always-on device.
